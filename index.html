The Pitmasters

Two state jobs, one smoker,
zero regrets.

Back in 2016, two guys traded their state desks for a trailer, a firebox, and a whole lot of oak. Fire Meats Wood was born on the simple idea that real barbecue can't be rushed — so we don't.

Everything rolls off the same live-fire smoker: brisket that rides 14 hours, ribs and pork cooked low and slow, then pushed into places barbecue doesn't usually go. Tacos. Fried rice. Burnt-end nachos. Family recipes with a little something extra.
